## Verdict

**The revision is not yet faithful, but the mathematics came through intact.** Compared with OLD, these are unchanged:
- every theorem, lemma, proposition and remark statement, both equations and every proof step;
- Tables I and II (the table bodies are byte-identical);
- every number and every Lean declaration name;
- every citation key except one.

Every cross-reference resolves to the right target. The core hedges survive: "up to a global phase", "up to a Pauli operator" (×4), "not formalized" (×2), "we have not found", the shared-code caveat for the two simulators, Davies–Jena conditional on Geelen, and the Dahlberg labeled/specified-pairs qualifiers.

**Three things must be fixed:**
- **M1:** the abstract dropped `for $n\ge 3$` from the characterization. Without it the statement is false for $K_2$, and it undoes an accepted Codex fix.
- **M2:** folding Related work into the introduction removed the credit to Löbl et al.'s exact fusion-count table for up to eight qubits. It also removed the Bui-Xuan et al. citation, which has now dropped out of `main.bbl`. Both were added as accepted review fixes.
- **M3:** the Acknowledgments gained a new claim that Codex confirmed the rewording changed no content. That is false for the current text.

There are also eleven smaller drifts in scope or hedging (S1–S11) that should be fixed. Everything else is an acceptable paraphrase or the intended restructuring.
