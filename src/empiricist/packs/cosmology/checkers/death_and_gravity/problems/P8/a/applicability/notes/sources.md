# Primary-source equation dictionary

Sources inspected on 2026-09-05. Our spectral reduction, proper-time correction
coefficients and Hardy comparison are independently derived in
[derivation.md](derivation.md).

[Fewster--Kontou, *Quantum strong energy inequalities*, arXiv:1809.05047v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.05047)
is the main QFT input. The unversioned PDF was checked to identify v2 dated
6 February 2019.

| Source | Use and retained condition |
|---|---|
| Section II | Free scalar field algebra; Hadamard states and smooth state differences |
| Lemma 4, Eq. (50), proof Eqs. (51)--(53) | Positive-type worldline bound for a real differential operator; applied on Minkowski eta>0 to D=partial_eta-1/eta, k=0 |
| Theorem 5 | Difference-QSEI framework; no replacement of reference stress by zero |

[Fewster--Kontou, *A semiclassical singularity theorem*, arXiv:2108.12668v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.12668v1),
Eqs. (13)--(20), supplies the EED/state-difference/SEE dictionary used by A.1.
Its Definition 3.1 remains an assumption for general curved backgrounds; the
present exact radiation calculation does not establish it generally.

[Lochan, *Unequal time Commutators in Friedmann universes*, arXiv:2208.13481v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.13481),
Section IV.B.1, Eqs. (54)--(55), independently supports the radiation reference
two-point function's rescaling from Minkowski space. We use only that field
relation, not the paper's broader backreaction conclusions. The minimally
coupled equation and Wronskian are replayed directly here.
